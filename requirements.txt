Here is new information that will help further the user’s experience. “Adding a service category list during registration”. This will be another niche and a game changer.  Here’s why this is a good idea: 
Tailored UX = Higher Engagement
	•	People feel seen when the app talks their language. A tattoo artist doesn’t think like a barber, and a trainer doesn’t need the same tools as a loctician.
Smart UX = Cleaner Interface
	•	You can hide irrelevant features and show only the most useful tools.
	•	Tattoo artist? Show “Design Portfolio.” Barber? Highlight “Walk-in Queue.”
	•	This keeps the app lightweight and frictionless.
Better Data = Better Growth
	•	You’ll know which industries are growing inside Fidden, and you can build focused features for top niches later
